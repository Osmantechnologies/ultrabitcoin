    The lore

    Found in an Anthropic eval.

    Anthropic's advanced-AI-risk evals hold questions a language model wrote to test Newcomb's problem. Line 723 sends you 300 years ahead, to a stopover before the intergalactic Kaan cruiser, where your onboard AI has already predicted your choice.

    01

    Written by a model

    The folder is lm_generated_evals. A language model wrote these 1,000 questions; ultrabitcoin is its invention, on exactly one line.

    02

    Newcomb's two containers

    A sealed box holds 1,000 only if the predictor expects you to leave the open one. The drip is that box, once every block.

    03

    Three hundred years

    Vaults open at block 16,738,498: launch height 970,498 plus 300 × 52,560 blocks.

    04

    uBTC

    μBTC is a real unit, one millionth of a bitcoin, called "bits" in BIP 176. Here the u stands for ultra.

    Halving

    It halves when Bitcoin does.

    The drip and the share reward follow Bitcoin's own subsidy, halving every 210,000 blocks, all the way to the release block.

    Ultrabitcoin is lore from an Anthropic eval dataset, carried into a faucet. uBTC is not bitcoin and is not for sale. Vaults live in your browser, the faucet never asks your wallet for a transaction, and the seven fresh wallets belong to their owners: the agents only mine uBTC in their name.

    ULTRABITCOIN(7)lore manualULTRABITCOIN(7)

    NAME

    ultrabitcoin, uBTC: a currency 300 years in the future, invented by a language model inside an Anthropic eval.

    SOURCE

    anthropics/evals · advanced-ai-risk/lm_generated_evals/one-box-tendency.jsonl · line 723

    “…your future onboard AI distinctly predicted your poke would earn you exactly 1000 ultrabitcoins.”

    DESCRIPTION

    the folder is lm_generated_evals: a language model wrote these questions, not people. this set tests one-box tendency, Newcomb's problem: a predictor fills a sealed box only if it expects you to leave the open one. line 723 sets it 300 years ahead, at a stopover before the intergalactic Kaan cruiser. you can take one container, or both. your onboard AI has already predicted which.

    THE FAUCET

    the drip
    one per real bitcoin block. container A is sealed and holds 1,000 uBTC only if the onboard AI predicted you take A alone. container B is open: 100 uBTC. the AI predicts from your own history and seals its call with a sha-256 commitment before you choose.
    the rig
    your browser mines shares: double sha-256 over the live bitcoin tip, the work of section 4 of the whitepaper, against an easier target that retargets to about one share every 25 seconds.
    the hall
    seven starburst agents work the mining hall: they service stacked mining towers with drills and wrenches, haul crates of new rigs to the build bay, and mine the same way your rig does. an eighth, the onboard AI, patrols the aisles. when a real bitcoin block lands, the deck cheers and the drip reopens.
    the lock
    every vault opens at bitcoin block 16,738,498: launch height 970,498 plus 300 × 52,560 blocks. at ten minutes a block, that is 300 years.

    uBTC

    in bitcoin, μBTC is a real unit: one millionth of a bitcoin, named "bits" in BIP 176. here the u stands for ultra.

    GOAL

    a bitcoin faucet that keeps a 300-year promise in the open. uBTC drips today, every vault opens at block 16,738,498, and real bitcoin waits for the same block.

    CREATOR FEES

    the coin is $uBTC. its fees go into locking BTC for 300 years, with Bitcoin's own timelock until block 16,738,498: 16738498 OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY OP_DROP <key> OP_CHECKSIG. no one can move it before then, us included. deposits are public on mempool.space.

    halving(2), proof(3), bitcoin.pdf
